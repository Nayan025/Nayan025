About me

Research with an engineering focus

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Electrical Engineering at The University of Texas at Dallas. My work combines electromagnetic design, computational modeling, and data-driven methods to improve antenna and RF-system performance. Before my doctoral studies, I held university teaching appointments in Bangladesh.

Research

Areas of interest

My research aims to connect electromagnetic insight with practical design methods for high-performance wireless and microwave systems.

AI

AI/ML-enabled antenna design

Interpretable machine learning, surrogate modeling, forward and inverse prediction, and data-driven antenna optimization.

↗

Phased arrays & beamforming

Adaptive beamforming, array performance, mutual-coupling reduction, and electronically directed radiation patterns.

mm

Millimeter-wave antennas and Gap waveguides based antenna for high power applications

Compact millimeter-wave antenna arrays and gap-waveguide-based antenna architectures for high-power and high-EIRP wireless applications.

RF

RF/microwave circuits

GaN-based microwave and millimeter-wave power amplifiers, efficiency, gain, and output-power trade-offs.

6G

Next-generation wireless

Advanced antenna and RF technologies for 5G/6G systems, wireless power transfer, and RF energy harvesting.
